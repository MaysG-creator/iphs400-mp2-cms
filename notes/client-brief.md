# Client brief — Kenyon College Men's Soccer

## Who runs the site, and how good are they with computers?
The site would be run by a media member of Kenyon College, there are many students that are involved with sports teams and they could run the site. We can assume the students have average knowledge of computers.
## Who reads it, and what are they usually looking for?
Fans of the team, alumni, parents and relatives of the players and relatives of coaches and any kenyon college student
## What's broken about how they do it now?
Most updates about the team come through social media sites like instagram which can be confusing for older people and could make the process of getting updates from the team more random. The website could make it more streamlined and easier for non tech-savy people.
## What would make them say "this is better"?
An easy to work interface and updates on the latest information given to them immediately so they won't have to go searching for it
## What must never happen?
the website should never have false information about the team such as game scores, scorers and other statistics. There should be only one person as an admin of the site.
