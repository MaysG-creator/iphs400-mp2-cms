History

Last updated October 14, 2026

Kenyon College Men's soccer began in 1947. The program had little success until 1990 when they made their first NCAA tournament appearance and first NCAC title win. In 1996, the Kenyon Men's Soccer Program advanced to the championship match of the DIII Men's Soccer NCAA tournament but unfortunately lost.
